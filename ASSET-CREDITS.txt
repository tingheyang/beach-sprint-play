Beach Sprint prototype — asset credits and implementation notes

Hands: WebXR Input Profiles Assets, generic-hand left.glb / right.glb.
https://github.com/immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles/tree/main/packages/assets
MIT license: assets/HANDS-LICENSE.txt. Each supplied hand has 25 joints.
Modified: bone hierarchy reconstructed, animated grasp poses, material replacement.

Three.js r170 and loader utilities: MIT, vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

Boat, oars, rig and smooth weighted arm geometry: authored for this prototype.
Boat is a smooth procedural 3D mesh, not a manufacturer scan or licensed boat model.
Arms: SkinnedMesh with shoulder and elbow bones; elbow pose solved from wrist target.
Hands: genuine supplied skinned mesh and articulated finger bones; not motion capture.
Materials: physically based clearcoat, normal maps and environment lighting.

Stamina: subtract 1.2 percentage points per accepted stroke, no passive regeneration.
Impulse: 1.45 * timingQuality * (0.2 + 0.8 * staminaBeforeStroke/100).
20% minimum impulse preserves finishability at zero stamina. Boat speed cap remains.
Run via local HTTP server; keep index.html, craft3d.js, vendor/ and assets/ together.

Grip linkage revision: hand roots are attached in the oar frame. Finger poses are fitted
to the cylindrical grip and held stable during the stroke. Limited wrist roll preserves
the same grip axis. Oarlock pivots remain fixed; recovery and blade feathering use
continuous eased phase curves. Back-view athlete/rig orientation is rotated together.
Surface audit: 10,616 vertex/edge/triangle-centre samples per hand. No sampled surface
point penetrates the 17.5 mm grip radius. This is a sampling audit, not an exhaustive
continuous triangle collision guarantee or a full anatomical validation.
